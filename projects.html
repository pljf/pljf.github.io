CaseCraft

A full-stack legal reasoning platform for law students to create case transcripts, choose prosecution or defense roles, and generate structured, AI-assisted arguments.

Node.js Express MongoDB Groq OpenAI
  • Built live, speaker-separated transcription from microphone and shared computer audio.
  • Integrated Groq and OpenAI speech-to-text APIs with automatic fallback.
  • Implemented authentication and persistent case management with Clerk, Google OAuth, Node.js, Express, and MongoDB.
Audience
Law students practicing legal reasoning from prosecution and defense perspectives.
Workflow
Case transcripts, role selection, and structured AI-assisted arguments.
Deployment
Vercel, Render, MongoDB Atlas, and Docker.

Full-stack / Jun 2026 – Present

TableSync

A collaborative meal-planning platform for sharing dietary preferences, voting on menus, and coordinating shopping across seven gathering formats.

Next.js React TypeScript PostgreSQL
  • Built constraint-based menu recommendations accounting for dietary restrictions, allergies, preferences, and budgets.
  • Added automatic ingredient scaling and cost-balanced grocery assignments to coordinate group shopping.
  • Implemented persistent rooms and host/guest access controls with PostgreSQL, Prisma, and Better Auth.
Planning
Shared dietary preferences, menu voting, and shopping coordination across seven gathering formats.
Access
Persistent rooms with host and guest access controls.
Testing
Unit, database integration, and end-to-end tests with Vitest and Playwright.
